Your tablet is a second monitor.

Extendesk adds a real extra screen to your Windows PC using the Android tablet or phone you already own. Drag windows onto it, touch it, hear your PC through it.

Get Extendesk How it works

Real extended desktop

Windows sees a genuine extra monitor, not a mirror. Arrange it, set its resolution and drag windows across.

USB or Wi-Fi

Plug in a cable for the lowest latency, or connect over your local network with no cable at all.

Touch and audio

Tap and scroll on the tablet to control the PC, and hear the PC's sound on the tablet.

How it works

  1. Install the Extendesk app on your Android device.
  2. Install Extendesk for Windows on your PC. It adds the virtual monitor.
  3. Open both. Choose your PC on the tablet, or just plug in the USB cable.

Requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and Android 8.0 or newer. Both devices must be on the same network for Wi-Fi, or connected with a USB cable.

Questions

Does it send my screen to the internet?

No. Your screen goes directly from your PC to your tablet, over USB or your local network. It never passes through our servers. See the privacy policy.
